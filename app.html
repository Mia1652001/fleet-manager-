Company details

These appear on invoices, reminder messages and exports. Anyone signed in to this company can edit them.

Your own rates for bookings taken in a foreign currency — the rate you actually give at the desk, margin included. When a rate is set, the booking form fills in the converted amount automatically (still editable). Leave a currency blank to type conversions by hand. Update these when the market moves enough to matter.

Receipts carry a consecutive number, which is what the MRA expects on a receipt. This is the letters in front of it: with "OCR" the numbers read OCR-2026-0001, OCR-2026-0002 and so on, restarting each January. Leave it blank for plain 2026-0001. Letters and digits only. Changing it later affects new receipts only — a receipt already issued keeps the number the customer was given.

What your bank takes on a credit-card payment, passed on to the client. This is only the default: it fills in on a booking when "Paying by credit card" is ticked, and can be changed there for a one-off. Bookings already saved keep the rate they were agreed at, so changing this never alters an invoice that has gone out. Leave blank if you never take cards.
